Ontology Specification Draft

Abstract

The AΙoD Metadata Model is designed to to sufficiently define the different entities relevant to the European AI ecosystem that will allow the coverage of those requirements and aims to facilitate interoperability with widely used models and external platforms that should be easily connectable to the AIoD platform.

Introduction back to ToC

The AIoD Metadata Model is developed to encapsulate the European AI ecosystem, which entails the incorporation of conceptualisations for various ecosystem aspects: informational, technical, networking, and educational. Its goals are to provide a structured hierarchy of AIoD Concepts and ensure interoperability with existing models and platforms.
