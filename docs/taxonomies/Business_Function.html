Business Function

Accounting and Finance

Definition: Accounting deals with record-keeping and reporting decisions for your company's finances. Finance helps to analyze all the data to make important decisions about how best to direct the organization.

Customer Service and Support

Definition: The team of Company's people who provide help when customers have trouble with a company's products or services.Required activities to become aware about the problems and troubles the company’s customers are facing about the products and services.

Human Resources (HR)

Definition: Departments focused on handling a broad spectrum of aspects related to managing people and attracting, selecting, and hiring candidates for open positions within the organization.

Legal & Compliance

Definition: The part of a company that understands the laws and also implements systems and processes to adhere to them consistently.

Marketing

Definition: The strategies and processes that generate persuasive prospects to buy a product or service

Operations

Definition: Activities for strategically and date to date management of company’s resources for making its processes

Production

Definition: A process of turning raw materials and ideas into products and services.

Purchasing and Procurement

Definition: Procurement is the strategic process of sourcing and acquiring goods and services, focusing on long-term benefits and relationships. Purchasing is the transactional component of this process and includes the act of buying these goods and services.

R&D Processes

Definition: The process of discovering new knowledge about products, services, or processes, and applying that knowledge to create new and improved products, services, or processes that fill market needs.

SCM & Distribution

Definition: The part of supply chain management that deals with the efficient forward and reverse flow of goods, services, and related information

Sales

Definition: All activities and processes involved in selling products or services. It includes prospecting, engaging with potential customers, managing sales teams, and closing deals.
